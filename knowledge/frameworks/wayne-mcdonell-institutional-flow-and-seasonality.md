_Channel: ForexToday_  

Why Do Central Banks Buy Gold: Balance sheet mechanics when a central bank buys $1B in gold. Assets +$1B gold (replaces $1B cash), liabilities -$1B cash - balanced. ## Core Methodology — The Three Layers

Every video in this corpus applies a consistent three-layer confluence before positioning:

1. **Institutional Flow** — Fed funds futures (trillions in negotiated interbank contracts),
   yield curve shifts, and Treasury market bid/demand reveal what smart money is pricing.
   Key insight: the yield curve tells you what's in the FOMC minutes before they're released.
   Distinguish *next meeting* (often ~100% priced) from *glide path* (future meetings).

2. **Seasonality** — Historical base rates by month, week, and event. Each calendar day/month
   has statistical tendencies (e.g., August skew, December CPI pattern). Build scenarios
   for 'better than expected' / 'worse than expected' / 'equal to consensus' before the event.

3. **Technical Price Action** — Only after layers 1+2 agree, confirm with pivot points,
   moving averages (especially 21 EMA on 4H), support/resistance, and candlestick patterns.
   Confluence zones: weekly + daily pivot overlap with MA and trend structure.

### Screening Pipeline

The daily workflow (see `daily_trading_routines_and_live_sessions` theme):

1. **Fundamental scoring** — Score every currency on: interest rates, jobs, inflation, GDP,
   seasonality, retail/institutional positioning.
2. **Hot / Cold / Neutral lists** — Pairs with divergent fundamentals go on hot/cold lists;
   neutral pairs are skipped entirely ('4,994 things that can waste my time today').
3. **Bulk chart sweep** — 2–3 minute pass through ~5,000 instruments; only patterns on
   the hot/cold lists get attention.
4. **Deep-dive checklist** — Weekly + daily pivots, 21 EMA on 4H, confluence zones.
5. **Trade plan** — Entry, exit, trigger, and stop all pre-committed before volatility.
